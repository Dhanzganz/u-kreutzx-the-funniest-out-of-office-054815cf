An out-of-office auto-reply that hands the whole office to Dave — who has no idea he's in charge now.

## Entry

> I'm out of the office until Monday. For urgent matters, contact Dave — he doesn't know yet, but he's in charge now.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single-line entry (115 characters, within the 140-character gate) to `/output/entry.txt`.
